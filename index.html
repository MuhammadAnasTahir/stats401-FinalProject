The Football Transfer Market

Zihan (Martina) Ma & Muhammad Anas Tahir

Composition Flows Performance

A visual report on where transfer money moves, who receives it, and whether it changes what happens on the pitch.

European football's transfer market is often discussed through record fees and individual deals. This project steps back to examine its wider structure across the five major leagues.

Market composition

Transfer expenditure is concentrated among a small group of clubs across Europe’s five major leagues.

Transfer flows

Transfer fees connect Europe’s top leagues with each other and with clubs across the rest of the world.

Spending and performance

Club investment can be compared with subsequent sporting performance to examine what transfer-market spending may deliver.
